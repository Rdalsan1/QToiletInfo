Quantified Toilets: What You Should Know

What is a Quantified Toilet?

The Quantified Toilet is a smart public toilet that uses built-in sensors to analyze waste and detect health-related information in real-time. These systems are designed to improve public health monitoring by identifying patterns in biological data.

What Information is Collected?

  • Biomarkers for drug use, pregnancy, or disease
  • Hydration and nutrient levels
  • Time of use
  • Toilet location and frequency of visits

How is the Data Used?

All data is anonymized and used to:

  • Identify public health trends
  • Inform sanitation and cleaning schedules
  • Support health-focused community initiatives

Privacy and Access

  • No personal or visual identification is collected
  • Only authorized agencies and researchers can view aggregate data
  • Consent options may be available via QR codes or linked apps

Quantified Toilets: What You Should Know

What is a Quantified Toilet?

The Quantified Toilet is a smart public toilet that uses built-in sensors to analyze waste and detect health-related information in real-time. These systems are designed to improve public health monitoring by identifying patterns in biological data.

What Information is Collected?

  • Biomarkers for drug use, pregnancy, or disease
  • Hydration and nutrient levels
  • Time of use
  • Toilet location and frequency of visits

How is the Data Used?

All data is anonymized and used to:

  • Identify public health trends
  • Inform sanitation and cleaning schedules
  • Support health-focused community initiatives

Privacy and Access

  • No personal or visual identification is collected
  • Only authorized agencies and researchers can view aggregate data
  • Consent options may be available via QR codes or linked apps

Opt Out of Data Collection

If you do not wish to have your data included, you can opt out below. This system respects your preference and allows you to control participation both digitally and physically.

Opt-Out in the Restroom

Inside each stall, you'll find a lever that controls your privacy settings. By default, the system is set to opt-in (green light). You can change your preference using this simple method:

  • Flip the lever UP to opt in to anonymous health data collection. (Light turns green)
  • Flip the lever DOWN to opt out. No data will be recorded. (Light turns red)

The lever is located near the base of the stall wall and can be easily adjusted with your hand. The light indicator is mounted at eye level to confirm your selection before use.

You'll also find a button on the floor, that controls your privacy settings. By default, the system is set to opt-in (green light). You can change your preference using this simple method:

  • Step on the button to opt out, No data will be recorded. (Light turns red)
  • Step on the button again to opt into anonymous health data collection (Light turns green)

The button is located near the base of the stall and can be easily adjusted with your foot. The light indicator is mounted at eye level to confirm your selection before use.
